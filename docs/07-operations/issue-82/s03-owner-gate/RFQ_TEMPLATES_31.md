# S03 RFQ templates — 31 positions

Status: `DRAFT_NOT_SENT`. No RFQ may be sent without a separate Owner dispatch authorization.

## Ingredient or purchased VSF

Required request fields: customer legal entity and delivery site; frozen BOM item and VKM scope; specification version; make-buy status; forecast and sample need. Supplier response must state legal entity, exact SKU/article/EAN, manufacturer, composition/allergens, net/drained mass, pack, price ex/in VAT, VAT rate, delivery, MOQ, lead time, validity, storage/transport, shelf life, sample availability and primary specification/conformity documents. A public product card is not a quotation.

## Packaging

Required request fields: channel, dish scope, fill mass/volume/temperature, holding time, orientation, demand and label requirements. Supplier must state exact SKU/material, useful dimensions/volume, unit/case quantity, temperature/food-contact compatibility, seal/tamper/stacking, conformity documents, price/VAT/delivery/MOQ/lead time and sample availability. Supplier evidence does not replace fill/leak/transport/quality testing.

## Equipment

Required request fields: function code, operations, dishes, approved peak demand/batch/cycle and site utility limits. Supplier must provide exact model/article, passport, nominal/useful capacity and test conditions, cycle, dimensions/service zones, electrical/water/drain/ventilation requirements, consumptions, completeness, installation/commissioning/training, warranty/service, price/VAT/delivery and demo test. Passport capacity is a manufacturer claim until site installation and timed load testing.

Prepared requests remain `NOT_SENT_NO_OWNER_AUTHORIZATION`.
